Delete your data

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Facts for Kids keeps your data on your device only. We do not store your favourites, progress, credits, or settings on our servers, and the app has no accounts.

Data stored on your device

  • Saved favourites (Premium), reading progress, daily-challenge progress, swipe credits, and app settings.
  • All of it is stored in the app's private storage on the device only — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How to delete it

  • Clear app data — Android Settings → Apps → Facts for Kids → Storage → Clear data. This removes all app data from the device.
  • Uninstall the app — this removes the app and its data from the device.
  • There is no cloud copy to delete, because nothing is stored on our servers.

Premium subscription

Premium is managed entirely by Google Play. To cancel or manage it, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. We never receive your payment details.

Advertising data

Advertising is served by Google AdMob, which may use an advertising ID as described in the Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. To request deletion of data held by Google, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Requesting deletion

If you have any other questions or want to request deletion of any data, email autoaccentsni@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
